# How we work

> A starting point. Every line below is a guess at how a small startup works. Change each one
> until it is true for you, and delete what you don't do. Part of [[07-Maps/Team]].

## The week
- **Monday, 30 minutes:** each person says the one thing they will finish this week. One thing,
  not a list.
- **Friday, 15 minutes:** what got finished, what did not, and why. No slides.

## The month
1. **First working day:** the person who keeps the books adds last month in the Monthly update
   space in Tamari: revenue, customers, cash in the bank and what we spent.
2. **Same week:** a founder writes the three notes (went well, did not go well, where investors
   can help), copies the update from Tamari into an email and sends it.
3. Mark the month as sent in Tamari. The Latest month widget shows it until then.
4. Anything we decided because of the numbers goes in [[06-Wiki/decision-log]].

## Who decides what
- **Product:** <name> decides what we build next. Everyone can argue; one person decides.
- **Money:** <name> approves any spend above <amount>.
- **Hiring:** nobody is hired without meeting everyone on the team.

## Talking to customers
- Everyone talks to at least one customer a month, not only the people who sell.
- A customer conversation becomes a note in `05-Knowledge/`, the same day, with what they said
  in their own words.
