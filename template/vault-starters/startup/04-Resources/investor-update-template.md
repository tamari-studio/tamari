# Investor update template

> The Monthly update space in Tamari writes the numbers and your three notes as plain text. This
> note says what to put around them. Keep the same order every month, so readers can compare.
> Part of [[07-Maps/Team]].

## Before you write
- Send it in the first week of the month, also after a bad month. A late update worries people
  more than a bad number.
- One screen long. If it needs scrolling twice, cut.

## The parts

**Subject:** <Company> update, <month year>

**One line first:** <the most important thing that happened, good or bad>

**Numbers** (copied from Tamari)
- Monthly revenue, with the change since last month
- Customers
- Cash in the bank, and how many months it lasts
- Spent this month

**What went well**
<!-- Two or three lines. Facts, not adjectives: "five new customers, three from referrals". -->

**What did not go well**
<!-- Always fill this in. Say what you will do about it. -->

**Where you can help**
<!-- One to three asks that someone can act on in five minutes: a name, a role, a question. -->

**Thank you**
<!-- Name the people who helped last month and what came of it. -->
