# Glossary

> One line per word, in the meaning it has *here*. Add a word the first time a new colleague or an
> assistant has to ask. Part of [[07-Maps/Team]].

| Word | What we mean by it |
|------|--------------------|
| Monthly revenue | What customers pay us every month for the product. One-off work does not count. |
| Customer | A company that paid us last month. A trial is not a customer yet. |
| Cash in the bank | The balance on the last day of the month, in one currency. |
| Spend | Everything that left the account in the month, salaries too. |
| Months of cash | Cash in the bank divided by what the month cost after revenue. Tamari works it out. |
| Investor update | The email we send every month with the numbers and three short notes. |
| Ask | One thing an investor can do for us, small enough to do in five minutes. |
| Pilot | A customer who tries the product for an agreed time and price before deciding. |
| Churn | A customer who stopped paying. We write down why. |
