# How we work

> A starting point. Every line below is a guess at how a small agency works. Change each one
> until it is true for you, and delete what you don't do. Part of [[07-Maps/Team]].

## The week
- **Monday, 30 minutes:** open the Clients space in Tamari together. Every lead and active client
  has a next step with a date. Overdue steps get a new date or a new owner before the meeting ends.
- **Friday:** each owner writes one line per client in its note: what happened, what comes next.

## Who owns a client
- Every client has one owner on our team. The owner is the person the client writes to.
- The owner keeps the client's row in Tamari current and its note in `02-Projects/` up to date.
- When the owner is away for more than three days, they name a stand-in in the client note.

## Taking on a client
1. Add the client in Tamari as a **Lead**, with the next step.
2. When they say yes: copy [[04-Resources/client-note-template]] into `02-Projects/<client>/README.md`
   and set the status to **Active**.
3. Agree on scope, price and the first delivery date in writing. Put the link in the client note.

## Handing work over
- A handover is a note, not a chat message: what is done, what is open, where the files are.
- The person who takes over reads it and asks questions the same day.

## Finishing
- When the work ends, set the client to **Finished** in Tamari and move its folder to `08-Archive/`.
- Write down one thing to do the same way next time and one thing to change, in the client note.
