# Glossary

> One line per word, in the meaning it has *here*. Add a word the first time a new colleague or an
> assistant has to ask. Part of [[07-Maps/Team]].

| Word | What we mean by it |
|------|--------------------|
| Lead | Someone we talk to who is not a client yet. In Tamari: status **Lead**. |
| Active | A client we are doing paid work for right now. |
| On hold | A client where work has stopped for now, with a date to check in again. |
| Finished | The work ended. The client can come back as a lead. |
| Owner | The one person on our team who looks after a client. |
| Next step | The one thing that has to happen next for a client, with a date. |
| Scope | What we agreed to deliver, in writing. Anything else is a new agreement. |
| Retainer | A fixed monthly fee for an agreed amount of work. |
| Handover | A written note that passes work from one person to another. |
