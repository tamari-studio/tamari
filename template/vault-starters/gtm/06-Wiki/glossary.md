# Glossary

> One line per word, in the meaning it has *here*. Add a word the first time a new colleague or an
> assistant has to ask. Part of [[07-Maps/Team]].

| Word | What we mean by it |
|------|--------------------|
| Deal | One company that might buy one thing from us. It has a card in Tamari. |
| Pipeline | All open deals together, and the space in Tamari that shows them. |
| Stage | Where a deal stands: New, Talking, Offer sent, Won or Lost. |
| Open | A deal that is New, Talking or Offer sent. |
| Value | What the deal is worth in its first year, in one currency. |
| Owner | The one person on our team who runs a deal. |
| Next step | The one thing that has to happen next on a deal, with a date. |
| Offer | The price and the scope, in writing, with a date it holds until. |
| Demo | A call where we show the product on their own example. |
| Pilot | A customer who tries the product for an agreed time and price before deciding. |
