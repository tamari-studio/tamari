# How we work

> A starting point. Every line below is a guess at how a small sales and marketing team works.
> Change each one until it is true for you, and delete what you don't do. Part of [[07-Maps/Team]].

## The week
- **Monday, 30 minutes:** open the Pipeline space in Tamari together and go through the open deals
  from right to left: Offer sent first, then Talking, then New. Every open deal has a next step
  with a date. Overdue steps get a new date or a new owner before the meeting ends.
- **Friday:** each owner writes one line per deal in its note: what happened, what comes next.

## What the stages mean
- **New:** they asked, or we found them. Nobody has talked yet. A new deal gets a reply within
  one working day.
- **Talking:** we have had a real conversation and know what they need.
- **Offer sent:** they have a price and a scope in writing. We call within three days.
- **Won:** they said yes in writing.
- **Lost:** they said no, or did not answer three times. Write the reason in the deal note.

## Who owns a deal
- Every deal has one owner on our team. The owner keeps its card in Tamari current.
- The value on the card is what the first year is worth. A guess is better than an empty field.

## From a won deal to a happy customer
1. Move the deal to **Won** in Tamari.
2. Copy [[04-Resources/deal-note-template]] into `02-Projects/<company>/README.md` if it is not
   there yet, and fill in what was promised.
3. The owner introduces the person who looks after the customer from now on, in one email.

## What we learn from lost deals
- Once a month, read the reasons of the lost deals together. A reason that comes up three times
  goes in [[06-Wiki/decision-log]] with what we will do about it.
