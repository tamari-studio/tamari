# Decision log

> Newest first. Write a decision down the day it is made: what, why, who, and what we chose not
> to do. A decision changes with a new entry, never by editing an old one. Part of
> [[07-Maps/Team]].

## How to add one
Copy the block below to the top of the list and fill it in. Five lines are enough.

```markdown
### YYYY-MM-DD: <the decision in one sentence>
- **Why:** <the reason, and what made it urgent now>
- **Decided by:** <name>
- **Instead of:** <what we chose not to do>
- **Look again:** <a date or an event, or "no">
```

## Decisions

### 2026-11-25: A draft with a gap left in it does not go to a reader
- **Why:** A made-up number in a published piece costs more trust than a late piece. The draft
  marks every missing fact as `[NEEDS: ...]`, so a gap is easy to see and easy to refuse.
- **Decided by:** <name>
- **Instead of:** Letting the reader catch the gaps while they read for tone.
- **Look again:** After ten pieces.
