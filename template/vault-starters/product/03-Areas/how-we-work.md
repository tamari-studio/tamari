# How we work

> A starting point. Every line below is a guess at how a small product team works. Change each
> one until it is true for you, and delete what you don't do. Part of [[07-Maps/Team]].

## Where requests come in
- Anyone who hears a request adds it in the Roadmap space in Tamari the same day: what they asked
  for, who asked, and the problem behind it in their words.
- Before adding, look for the same request. If it is there, select **+1** instead.
- We write down the problem, not the solution. "The bookkeeper types bookings into a sheet by
  hand" tells us more than "add an export button".

## The week
- **Tuesday, 30 minutes:** go through the new requests, the most asked first. Each one gets an
  answer: **Plan it** or **Not now**. Nothing stays new for more than two weeks.
- **Friday:** move what went live to **Shipped** and tell the people who asked.

## How we decide
- A request moves up when many people ask, when the people asking are the customers we want
  more of, and when the work is small.
- <name> decides what goes from Planned to In progress. Everyone can argue; one person decides.
- We work on at most <number> things at a time. A new one starts when another one ships.

## Before we build
- Anything bigger than a few days gets a short brief first: copy
  [[04-Resources/feature-brief-template]] into `02-Projects/<feature>/README.md`.

## Saying no
- **Not now** is an answer, and the person who asked hears it from us with the reason.
- A decision not to build something that people keep asking for goes in
  [[06-Wiki/decision-log]], so we don't have the same discussion every month.
