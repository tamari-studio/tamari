# Glossary

> One line per word, in the meaning it has *here*. Add a word the first time a new colleague or an
> assistant has to ask. Part of [[07-Maps/Team]].

| Word | What we mean by it |
|------|--------------------|
| Run of show | The plan for the event day, minute by minute: what happens, where, and who is on it. It lives in Tamari. |
| Item | One line in the run of show. |
| Owner | The one person on our team who makes an item happen and ticks it off. |
| Late | An item that is past its start time and not ticked off. |
| Event lead | The one person who decides when the plan has to change on the day. |
| Doors | The time guests can come in. Everything before it is set-up. |
| Set-up | Everything that happens before doors: building, sound check, badges. |
| Partner | A company that pays or brings something to be part of the event. |
| Supplier | A company we pay: venue, catering, sound, transport. |
| Wrap-up | The meeting within a week after the event: what to keep and what to change. |
