# How we work

> A starting point. Every line below is a guess at how a small event team works. Change each one
> until it is true for you, and delete what you don't do. Part of [[07-Maps/Team]].

## From idea to event day
1. **Three months before:** date, venue and budget are agreed in writing. Copy
   [[04-Resources/event-note-template]] into `02-Projects/<event>/README.md`.
2. **One month before:** the programme is fixed. Changes after this day need a yes from the
   event lead.
3. **Two weeks before:** the run of show is in Tamari, every item with a time, a place and an owner.
4. **The day before:** set-up and checks. They are items in the run of show too.

## The week before
- **Daily, 15 minutes:** read the run of show from the top. Each owner says "ready" or what is
  missing.
- Every supplier has confirmed their arrival time in writing.

## On the day
- The run of show in Tamari is the plan. If it is not in there, it does not happen.
- The owner ticks an item off when it has happened. A late item is the owner's to solve or to
  hand to the event lead.
- **One person decides** when the plan has to change: the event lead. They change the run of
  show first, then tell the team.
- Nobody argues with a guest. Send them to <name>.

## After the event
- **Within two days:** thank-you messages to speakers, partners and volunteers.
- **Within a week:** 45 minutes together: what to keep, what to change. The answers go in the
  event note, and anything that changes how we work goes in [[06-Wiki/decision-log]].
- Move the event's folder to `08-Archive/` when the last invoice is paid.
